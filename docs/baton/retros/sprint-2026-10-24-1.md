- Spike #41 tested the issue's hypothesis against transcripts before any code was written. It found `gh` reads under 3% of orchestrator context, so #43 shrank to the one criterion the data supported.

- Task sizes stayed small: no developer, refiner or reviewer agent passed 71K tokens at peak.

## How to improve

1. Repeat spike #42's measurement after a Baton release that carries #45, with at least three runs per role. Belongs in a new issue, for the maintainer to prioritise.
2. Do not give a spike an acceptance criterion that edits another author's issue; have the orchestrator's refiner apply findings. Belongs in Baton (`baton:sprint-plan` / `baton:refine`).
3. Consider a first-call view in `baton usage` (proposed by #42's findings). Belongs in a new issue.
